# Trimurti Coolers brand notes

## Identity

Use **Trimurti Coolers** as the customer-facing name. Trimurti Enterprise and its other businesses are out of scope for this website.

The regenerated TE symbol and wordmark live in `public/brand/`. They are deliberately simple vector files so they remain crisp at small header sizes and larger print or signage sizes.

## Voice

Clear, local and practical. Lead with the customer's space and cooling requirement, then explain the matching Novamax model. Keep calls to action enquiry-led: `Explore models`, `View specifications`, `Ask on WhatsApp`.

## Visual direction

The page uses a technical-editorial direction: cool blue structure, generous white space and genuine manufacturer product imagery. Product cards and product detail views keep the product image on a clean white stage without decorative grids. Avoid public price banners, marketplace badges, glossy gradients and generic stock coolers.

## Product content

Product cards may show model name, range, intended setting and verified technical details. Capacity variants stay as distinct models even when their photo is similar. Never add price, stock, ratings or unverified promises to a product card.
